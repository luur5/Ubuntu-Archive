About Ubuntu Archive

Ubuntu Archive is an AI-powered platform dedicated to preserving African oral histories, indigenous knowledge, and cultural heritage. Through technology, communities can record, translate and archive stories for future generations.

Our Vision

To become Africa's largest digital archive of oral history and indigenous knowledge.

Our Mission

To preserve African voices by making storytelling, translation and archiving accessible to everyone.
