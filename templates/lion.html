The Lion and the Hare

A traditional African folktale about wisdom, intelligence, and courage.

Once upon a time, a powerful lion ruled over the animals in the forest. The lion was feared by everyone because of his strength and size. However, a clever little hare was not easily frightened.

The hare used his intelligence to outsmart the lion and showed that wisdom can sometimes be more powerful than physical strength.

Moral of the Story

Intelligence, courage, and wisdom can overcome even the greatest challenges.
